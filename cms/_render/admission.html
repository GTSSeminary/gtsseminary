Admission to GTS

Admission is open to both male and female, of any age and any religious persuasion. If you truly desire to be a servant of God, we welcome your application.

Begin Your Application Read the FAQ

Admission Policy

Who can apply

Admission is open to both male and female students of any age and of any religious persuasion. GTS welcomes applications from anyone who truly desires to be a servant of God, regardless of background.

Any age

There is no upper or lower age barrier to beginning your studies.

Any religious persuasion

Men and women from all backgrounds are welcome to apply.

Training is tuition-free

GTS is committed to removing financial barriers to faithful preparation. Our seminary training is offered free of tuition, so that the call to ministry is not hindered by cost.

Requirements

Before you apply

The following registration requirements must be met by the student before consideration of acceptance to GTS.

  1. 1 Secondary education Completion of secondary school or an equivalent educational level. The applicant must be able to read and write.

    Secondary education

    Completion of secondary school or an equivalent educational level. The applicant must be able to read and write.

  2. 2 Registration forms The applicant must duly fill out registration forms and submit them accordingly.

    Registration forms

    The applicant must duly fill out registration forms and submit them accordingly.

  3. 3 Letter of recommendation A letter of recommendation from a church officer approving the student's desire to study at GTS. Exceptions may be considered on a case-by-case basis by the board of the seminary.

    Letter of recommendation

    A letter of recommendation from a church officer approving the student's desire to study at GTS. Exceptions may be considered on a case-by-case basis by the board of the seminary.

  4. 4 Two references The applicant must send in two references with the following information: full name, profession, designation, home or office address, email, phone number, and relationship to applicant.

    Two references

    The applicant must send in two references with the following information: full name, profession, designation, home or office address, email, phone number, and relationship to applicant.

How to Apply

Applying to Grace & Truth

1

Submit your application

Complete the registration forms and submit them along with your references and letter of recommendation.

2

Application review

Your application will be evaluated by the seminary.

3

Interview

An interview will be conducted by one or more members of the board of GTS.

What happens next?

All admitted students will be contacted, and all names published on the GTS website. We look forward to walking with you toward faithful ministry.
