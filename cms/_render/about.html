About Grace & Truth

A Reformed-Baptist seminary (1689 Confession) in Lagos, Nigeria, committed to preparing men and women for faithful ministry.

Who We Are

GTS is a Reformed-Baptist seminary, welcoming applications from anyone who truly desires to be a servant of God.

Holding to the 1689 Baptist Confession of Faith, GTS exists to train men and women for faithful ministry. Through our partner, Grace Bible University (GBU), we offer the Certificate of Ministry, the Associate Degree of Ministry, and the Bachelor's degrees in Theology and Ministry.

Faithful to Scripture. Scripture is held to be the supreme authority in every area of life — the individual, the family, the church, society, and government.

Open to all. Admission is open to both male and female, of any age and any religious persuasion.

Biblically tailored. Our training program is tailored according to the Bible's guidelines for ministry preparation — informing our purpose, curriculum, and faculty.

Introduction & Purpose

Why GTS exists

The world is in desperate need of faithful ministers of the gospel — men and women who know the Word of God, love the Lord Jesus Christ, and are able to teach others also. GTS exists to answer that need.

Our purpose is to prepare servants of God according to the Bible's own guidelines for ministry preparation. We believe God has given the church a clear pattern for training its leaders, and we seek to follow that pattern faithfully.

"Commit these to faithful men who will be able to teach others also." — 2 Timothy 2:2

Our Mission

A three-pronged, biblical approach

Our method of preparing men and women is grounded in Scripture itself.

1

Doctrine

Sound biblical teaching for the mind, so that students know the truth and can defend it. (2 Timothy 3:16–17)

2

Meditation

Personal reflection that shapes the heart, cultivating a life of prayer and devotion to Christ. (1 Corinthians 2:6–16)

3

Mentoring

Faithful training under godly mentors, preparing students to entrust the Word to the next generation. (2 Timothy 2:2; Matthew 28:19–20)

Philosophy of Education

A Christian theistic world-and-life view

GTS adopts the philosophy of education of Grace Bible University, which is based upon a Christian theistic world-and-life view — an inclusive philosophy of life that derives consistently from the Scriptures.

This is the first and overshadowing presupposition for a truly biblical approach to education. There is no higher authority for man in knowledge and reasoning. God is absolute, and his revelation comes to man with absolute authority; it is the inspired, infallible, inerrant, and all-sufficient Word of God.

The supreme authority

Scripture is held to be the supreme authority in every area of life for the individual, the family, the church, society, and government.

(2 Timothy 3:16)

From truth to life

Education is not merely the transfer of information; it is the formation of a whole person whose thinking and living are brought into line with the truth of God.

For the whole of life

Our philosophy touches every course we teach and every relationship we build — because the Christian worldview is an inclusive philosophy of life.

Confession of Faith

The 1689 Baptist Confession

GTS is a Reformed-Baptist seminary that holds faithfully to the Second London Baptist Confession of Faith (1689).

Reformed

Committed to the historic Reformed doctrines of grace — God's sovereignty in salvation and his glory in all things.

Baptist

Convicted of believers' baptism and the congregational government of the local church according to Scripture.

Confessional

Standing in the stream of historic Baptist confessional theology, teaching what faithful Baptists have believed for centuries.

Our Partner

Grace Bible University

GTS partners with Grace Bible University (GBU), through whom our certificates and degrees are awarded upon completion of course requirements. GTS has fully adopted the study methodology of GBU, which has developed a unique method of study combining doctrinal instruction, personal meditation, and structured mentoring.

Working together to equip the next generation of faithful servants of God.
