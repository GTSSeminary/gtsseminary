About GTS

What is Grace & Truth Theological Seminary (GTS)?

GTS is a Reformed-Baptist seminary holding to the 1689 Baptist Confession of Faith. It is a ministry of Sovereign Grace Bible Church, Lagos, and is affiliated with Grace Bible University (USA).

We welcome applications from anyone who truly desires to be a servant of God. Through our partner GBU, GTS offers the Certificate of Ministry, the Associate Degree of Ministry, and the Bachelor's degrees in Theology and Ministry.

Learn more about GTS
What does "Reformed-Baptist (1689 Confession)" mean?

GTS stands in the historic Baptist tradition, affirming the Reformed doctrines of grace as summarized in the Second London Baptist Confession of Faith (1689). This shapes our theology, our philosophy of education, and everything we teach.

Who can attend GTS?

Admission is open to both male and female students, of any age and of any religious persuasion. If you truly desire to be a servant of God, we welcome your application.

What is the relationship between GTS and Grace Bible University (GBU)?

GTS is affiliated with Grace Bible University (USA), through whom our certificates and degrees are awarded upon completion of course requirements. GTS has fully adopted GBU's unique study methodology, combining doctrine, meditation, and mentoring.

Programs & Academics

What programs does GTS offer?

GTS offers four programs through Grace Bible University:

  • Certificate of Ministry — 1 year, 30 units
  • Associate Degree of Ministry — 2 years, 60 units
  • Bachelor's Degree of Theology — 4 years, 120 units
  • Bachelor's Degree of Ministry — 4 years, 120 units

The credits of each degree can be applied toward the next higher degree.

Explore the programs
Do you offer a master's program? What about the CBTS master's program?

Yes. Beyond our certificate and bachelor's programs, GTS connects students with master's-level study through our partnership with Covenant Baptist Theological Seminary (CBTS) — a Reformed Baptist seminary offering graduate programs including the Master of Arts (M.A.) in fields such as Pastoral Studies, Biblical Studies, and Reformed Baptist Studies, the Master of Divinity (M.Div.), and the Master of Theology (Th.M.).

CBTS is designed for students preparing for faithful, church-centered ministry in the Reformed Baptist tradition, and its courses can be taken by students anywhere — with live-streamed and self-paced options.

Details on CBTS admission requirements and how GTS students apply will be added here. Contact the seminary for the current pathway.

What is the study method at GTS?

GTS has fully adopted the study methodology of GBU. Each course uses a Student's Study Guide workbook, and students engage through four elements:

  • Reflection & Discussion — one-to-one interaction with a mentor, grounded in a Christian worldview
  • Scriptural Memory Work — memorising and reciting the passages of Scripture for each course
  • Personal Application — practical application of the material to life
  • Self-Assessment — a private examination of one's own heart (2 Cor. 13:5)
See the study method
How long does each program take?

The Certificate of Ministry is a 1-year program (30 units), the Associate Degree of Ministry is a 2-year program (60 units), and each Bachelor's degree is a 4-year program (120 units). Because credits roll forward, a student can progress from the Certificate to a Bachelor's along one seamless pathway.

Admission & Application

When can students apply to GTS?

Applications open ahead of each academic year. For the 2026 academic year, applications open in February and the deadline is 30 March 2026, followed by board interviews and admission letters in April. Check the important dates page for the current calendar, or contact the seminary for exact deadlines.

What are the admission requirements?
  • Completion of secondary school or an equivalent educational level (the applicant must be able to read and write).
  • Duly filled registration forms submitted accordingly.
  • A letter of recommendation from a church officer approving the student's desire to study at GTS.
  • Two references providing full name, profession, designation, home or office address, email, phone number, and relationship to the applicant.
View full requirements
How do I apply?

Complete the registration forms, submit them with your two references and your letter of recommendation. Your application will be evaluated, and an interview will be conducted by one or more members of the board of GTS. All admitted students are contacted, and their names are published on the GTS website.

See how to apply
Do I need a letter of recommendation?

Yes. A letter of recommendation from a church officer approving the student's desire to study at GTS is required. Exceptions may be considered on a case-by-case basis by the board of the seminary.

Can I study at GTS if I am not a Baptist?

Admission is open to students of any religious persuasion. GTS teaches faithfully within the Reformed-Baptist tradition, and welcomes applications from anyone who truly desires to be a servant of God.

Fees & Costs

Is GTS tuition-free?

Yes. GTS is committed to removing financial barriers to faithful preparation, and our seminary training is offered free of tuition. Please contact the seminary regarding any registration or material fees that may apply.
