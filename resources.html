Library

A library for faithful study

Students at GTS are encouraged to build the habit of wide, careful reading. Here we point to the classic works of Reformed and Baptist theology that have served the church for centuries.

  • The Baptist Confession of Faith (1689) — the doctrinal standard of the seminary.
  • Confessions and catechisms of the Reformed tradition, for families and churches.
  • Expository and systematic theologies recommended by our faculty.
  • A selection of biographies, including the life of George Müller, for encouragement in faith.
